## 🔭 What I want to work on

**Altruistic, whole-stack systems.** AI is making software cheap to write. That's a rare opening: when rewriting a layer stops being a multi-year project, we can finally question the boundaries between layers instead of tuning inside them. I want to look at the stack as one connected system, from hardware and storage up to the applications on top, and build things that benefit everyone who runs on it, not just one benchmark.

**Novel over incremental.** Too many systems papers explore a well-mapped design space one more time. I'd rather chase the idea that redraws the map, and finish with something the whole community can pick up and build on.

**Close to the hardware.** SSDs, NVMe, CXL, GPUs: the hardware is where cost and energy are actually decided. I want to work close enough to it to cut both, not just to move the bottleneck somewhere else.
